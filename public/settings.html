Automatic website access

Pond can read ordinary websites after you grant access once. Browser settings, extension stores and other restricted pages remain unavailable.

Your area

Optional. Enter a city and state to match local resources. Pond never uses an article’s location as yours.

Understand what you’re reading

When enabled, Pond sends a short extract (up to 16,000 characters), selected metadata and the site’s domain to Pond’s hosted service and Anthropic to identify civic issues. No full URL, HTML, form fields or user location is sent. Text can still contain personal information.

No account or API key needed. You control website access.
